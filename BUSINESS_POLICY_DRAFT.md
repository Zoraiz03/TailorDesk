# TailorDesk business policy — DRAFT FOR OWNER REVIEW

This draft describes the intended commercial model. It is not a final contract, price list, warranty, or legal guarantee. Agree the final terms with the buyer before delivery; obtain appropriate advice for applicable legal obligations.

## Purchase and maintenance

- A one-time purchase provides perpetual use of the application releases covered by the signed license, for the agreed number of installations. A release is entitled when its sequence is at most the license's maximum release sequence.
- Annual maintenance covers the updates and support specifically agreed with the buyer. Its scope and renewal terms must be written down; do not promise every future feature or unlimited support.
- Maintenance expiry does not disable an already entitled release. Installing a later release may require renewed entitlement, imported before updating the app.
- Custom features, integrations, data work and other work outside the agreed package are quoted separately and require agreement before work starts.

## Phones and activation

- A replacement phone requires a new activation request and license reissuance for that installation. Confirm purchase, authorisation and the agreed installation count before reissuing.
- Offline replacement cannot remotely revoke a license on the old installation. Record the replacement reason and agree how the old installation is retired; do not claim technical remote revocation.
- A business backup transfers records, not activation. The destination must be activated separately before restoring business data.

## Business records and backups

- Shop owners retain ownership of their business records. Software branding does not replace the shop's identity on its receipts.
- Records are stored locally. Shop owners must arrange regular backups outside the device and protect access to the unencrypted backup files.
- TailorDesk has no automatic cloud synchronisation. A lost or damaged phone can mean lost records if no usable external backup exists.
- Any support access to records or backups should be agreed with the shop and limited to the work requested. Record the agreed handling/return/deletion arrangements separately.
