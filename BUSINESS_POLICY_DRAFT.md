# TailorDesk business policy — draft for owner review

This preview is for private testing and is not a production sale, contract, warranty, or public release. Pricing, support, maintenance, refunds, supported Android versions, and printer/equipment scope remain owner decisions.

Shop owners retain ownership of their business records. Records are stored locally; backups are unencrypted and must be exported regularly to protected storage outside the device. TailorDesk has no automatic cloud synchronisation. Any support access to records or backups must be explicitly agreed and limited to the requested work.
