## Handover

- [ ] Source, version, checksum and signing fingerprint checked by owner.
- [ ] Correct production identity and credentials; no TEST artifact delivered.
- [ ] Preview opens directly without an activation screen.
- [ ] Shop setup and receipt shop identity checked.
- [ ] Customer, measurements, order, advance/balance payment and delivery training completed.
- [ ] Karigar assignment and PDF workflow demonstrated.
- [ ] Backup exported and its arrival outside the phone demonstrated.
- [ ] Backup custodian, location and schedule agreed.
- [ ] Restore explained using isolated sample data only.
- [ ] Update guide explained, including backup first and retaining the installed app.
- [ ] Maintenance/support scope and outstanding commercial terms agreed in writing.
