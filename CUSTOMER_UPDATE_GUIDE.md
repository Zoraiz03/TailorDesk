# Updating TailorDesk

An update is installed over the TailorDesk app already on your Android phone. Keep the existing app and its data in place throughout these steps.

1. **Check with the owner first.** In **More → Settings**, find your license ID and “Entitled through release” value. Ask the owner whether your license covers the release sequence of the new app. The version number and license release sequence are different numbers.
2. **Renew entitlement first if needed.** Receive the replacement license from the owner and use **More → Settings → Import renewed license** in your existing app. Confirm it says **Activated**, then close and reopen the app. Maintenance expiry alone does not stop use of a release already covered by your license.
3. **Export a backup.** Use **More → Backup & Restore → Create and export backup**. Copy the file outside the phone and check it arrived. Note your installation code from the license screen, shop name and a recent customer/order/payment so you can compare them afterward.
4. **Receive the correct APK.** Get it from the owner and confirm its source and version. The owner must supply the same app identity and signing identity as your existing installation, with a higher Android version code.
5. **Install the update.** Open the APK and accept the phone's update/install prompt for the existing TailorDesk app. If prompted, allow installation only from the chosen source. Never uninstall TailorDesk or clear its data as a normal update step. If Android appears to be adding a separate app, stop and ask the owner.
6. **Check your shop.** Reopen TailorDesk. Confirm activation, the same installation code, shop settings, customer records, measurements, recent orders and recorded payments. Check **More → About TailorDesk** for the delivered app version.
7. **If installation fails, contact support.** Keep the old app, your backup and the new APK. Send the exact error message and your phone model/Android version through the agreed support channel. Do not uninstall, clear storage, restore over live records, or try an older app on your own.

Keep the external backup until you and the owner have finished checking the update. See [installation and backup instructions](CUSTOMER_INSTALLATION_GUIDE.md).
